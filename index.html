MEET THE ARTIST

Pradeesh K Raman

Kannur, Kerala, India

Pradeesh K Raman is a self-taught artist with a diploma in photography. Born in Kannur, Kerala, his brightly coloured paintings often explore contemporary figurative couples. Clean, expressive forms stand out against colourful surroundings filled with flowers, patterns and complementary shades.

His work is known for its simplicity, expressive character and delightful visual appeal.
